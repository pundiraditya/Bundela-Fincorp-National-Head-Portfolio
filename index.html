About Me

Shaping the Future of Finance

With over 3.4 years of hands-on experience in the NBFC sector, I specialize in driving business growth through strategic partnerships, credit distribution, and innovative green financing solutions — with a sharp focus on the rapidly evolving Electric Vehicle ecosystem.

As Business Development Manager (National) and Partner at Bundela Fincorp, I lead initiatives that bridge the gap between capital and sustainable mobility. My work spans end-to-end from origination to distribution, building networks that scale.

I believe the EV revolution is not just a technological shift — it's a financial one. Enabling the right financing architecture is what accelerates adoption at scale, and that's where I thrive.
